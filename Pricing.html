// Questions

Good to know

Do I need a domain name?

Yes, like yourshop.com. I'll help you pick and set it up. Domains usually cost about $12–20 a year and are paid directly to the registrar, so you own it.

I already use Booksy, Square, or DoorDash.

Perfect, keep them. Your website becomes the front door that sends customers to the booking or ordering you already use.

Can I update the site myself?

You can, or the Care Plan covers small updates for you. Either way, the site and its content belong to you.

Not sure which package fits?

Pick "Not sure yet" on the contact form. I'll recommend the smallest package that gets the job done.
